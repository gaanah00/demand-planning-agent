an impact. \\

& It parses the event into a \textbf{draft} scenario: 10 SKUs, 2

pass-through, FIFO timing, no demand effect). \textbf{Nothing is applied
yet.} \\

changes, what is affected, mitigations. Show the \textbf{Computed /

Answer shows ``Could not reach the Anthropic API'' & Switch to the phone
hotspot and click the last question chip again. The app keeps running,
and nothing needs restarting. \\
Rate limit or overload message & Wait 10 seconds and resend. \\
A chat answer is slow (\textgreater{} 60 s) & Talk through the progress
steps shown on screen. Forecasts are cached, so a repeated question is
instant. \\
Charts don't render & Restart with \texttt{uv\ run\ python\ start.py}
(plotly.js is served locally from the Python package). \\
The API is unusable for the whole session & Restart in \textbf{scripted
backup mode}. It runs the same tools and data with a scripted stand-in
for Claude, and the header shows ``model scripted-test-client''.
\textbf{Say so to the audience.} Windows PowerShell:
\texttt{\$env:DPA\_LLM\_STUB="e2e\_stub:make";\ \$env:PYTHONPATH="tests";\ uv\ run\ python\ start.py}.
The demo prompts above (data quality, forecast, India, South Korea,
confirm) are supported. \\
Laptop fails & Present screenshots from the dry run (take them during
the dry run). \\
